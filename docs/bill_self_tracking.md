# Household bill self-tracking resources

Verified from published provider pages on 2026-09-20.

- [Dominion Manage Account](https://www.dominionenergy.com/mya) states that
  Virginia customers can view hourly, daily, and monthly energy use.
- [Dominion Detailed Energy Use](https://www.dominionenergy.com/en/About/Delivering-Energy/Electric-Projects/Smart-Meter-Upgrades/Detailed-Energy-Use)
  states that customers with a communicating smart meter can enroll and
  download 30-minute interval data. Dominion notes that portal data can differ
  from validated billing data.
- [NOVEC SmartHub](https://www.novec.com/NOVEC-SmartHub.cfm) provides billing
  history and usage trends through **My Usage → My Usage → Usage Explorer**.
- [U.S. Department of Energy Green Button](https://www.energy.gov/data/green-button)
  explains standardized downloads of customer-authorized utility usage data
  when supported by the utility.

These tools help residents observe their own billed usage and charges. They
cannot identify a data center's causal share of any change.
